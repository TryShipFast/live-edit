Three chairs, one rule, since 2017.

Dev Okafor opened Third Chair in a former bike shop on Tanner Row with two booked chairs and one kept free for anyone who walked in. The rule stuck.

Today there are four barbers and still three chairs at the front. We cut every hair type, we don't upsell, and we'll tell you if the photo you brought won't work on your hair.
