# 32 Third Chair — section rationale (18 Oct 2026)
Not tested with real users. Reasoned from the category brief, market patterns and standard UX principles. Each row: who it serves · business job · likely reaction / risk → fix · metric Phase 2 can track.

## Shared
| Section | Visitor | Business job | Reaction / risk → fix | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Header (Book always visible) | everyone | bookings | Book is the most-wanted action; the risk is it competing with nav → red fill, nav plain | header Book clicks |
| Sticky Book/Call bar (mobile) | phone visitors | bookings, fewer missed calls | thumb reach; may cover content → 64px bar, page bottom padding | bar taps |
| Footer (hours, address, fictional note) | everyone | trust, fewer "are you open?" calls | hours are the 2nd most-checked fact | — |

## Home 1 — walk-in / passer-by
| Section | Visitor | Business job | Reaction / risk → fix | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crossfade shop hero | passer-by deciding now | footfall | shows the actual room; motion can distract → slow 6s fade, stops with reduced-motion | scroll past hero |
| Chair board + hold-my-spot | walk-in | fills idle chairs, cuts queue no-shows | answers "how long is the wait?"; risk of fake-looking data → Sample label, 20-min-per-person maths | holds submitted |
| Price columns | price-checker | fewer "how much?" calls | prices early build confidence; tables avoided per house rule → three columns | price section views → Book |
| Filmstrip of work | style-seeker | proof of skill | horizontal scroll hides items → visible arrows + partial next tile | gallery clicks |
| Barber rail | picky client | books named barbers (higher loyalty) | — | "Book with" clicks |
| Hours | walk-in | fewer calls | today highlighted | — |
