# 32 Third Chair — voice
Short, plain, a bit dry. Talks like the barber, not the brand. Times and prices first.
- Say: "Walk in. Chair three is kept free." / "Skin fade, 45 minutes." / "Running 10 minutes behind? We'll text."
- Avoid: "experience", "elevate", "gentleman's sanctuary", emojis, exclamation marks.
- Numbers in figures; prices always "Sample" in the footer note and on price blocks.
