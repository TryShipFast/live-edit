# 33 Lacquer Room — section rationale (rev1, 18 Oct 2026)
Each section: who it serves, what it earns for the studio, expected visitor response, risk + fix, and a Phase 2 measure. **Reasoned from the brief, category research and UX principles — not tested with real users.**

## Home 1 — shade hunter
| Section | Visitor | Business | Expected response | Risk → fix | Measure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hero: almond-cropped photo mosaic, headline left | shade hunter | sets the craft tone | "they do the look I want" | crops hide detail → work photos only | scroll past hero |
| Colour chart (finish × family → shade → price/time/wear → book) | shade hunter | upsell finish (classic → gel → BIAB/chrome) | explores, then books a specific shade | choice overload → 6 families, 6–8 shades each, empty state for Chrome × French | chart → book clicks per finish |
| Almond price tiles per finish | price checkers | fewer "how much" messages | compares by finish, sees duration + wear | tile text cramped → 2 columns on phone | tile taps |
| Gallery mosaic with finish tags | browsers | proof of skill | taps through to gallery | repetition → 6 newest only | gallery visits |
| Offset quotes (Sample) | doubters | trust | reads 1–2 | looks fake → labelled Sample, first names only | — |
| Gift a treatment chooser | gift buyers | voucher revenue | picks a named treatment (more personal than an amount) | price confusion → price shown per pick | voucher requests |

## Inner pages
Services (category cards + chart), treatment ×4 (what happens, duration, wear, price, aftercare, book), team + person ×4 (on-leave state), gallery (filter, Press-on empty, lightbox), gift vouchers (form error/sending/done), book (4 steps, deposit + patch-test note), about, contact, policies (7), careers (desk rental + apply), journal + posts (long title, no image), privacy, 404 — each routes back to Book.

Brief vs house rules: the brief suggests a dotted price list; house rule (cards, tables only where needed) wins.
