Why Maren

Ines Varga opened Maren in 2014 after twelve years in busy city salons where appointments ran to the minute. She wanted the opposite: a consultation before every service, prices you could read before you sat down, and stylists who were paid well enough to stay.

Twelve years on there are five of us. We close on Mondays so the whole team can train together, we patch test every colour client, and we will tell you if what you want will take more than one visit.

What we collect

When you send a booking request, a voucher request, a message or a job application, we receive what you type into the form: your name, email, phone number and anything you add. Booking requests also include the service, stylist and time you chose.

What we do with it

We use it to reply to you and to manage your appointment. Booking and voucher details are passed to our booking system, which has its own privacy notice. We do not sell your details, and this website does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

Messages and applications for twelve months. Client records, including patch test results, for as long as you are a client and two years after.
