## Voice
- Plain and warm; second person ("you"), short sentences.
- Say the time and the price up front ("About 3 hours · from $190 · Sample").
- No hype words (stunning, luxury, transform). No exclamation marks.
- Policies written as promises, not threats ("We hold your time for 15 minutes").

## Imagery
Real work in natural light: hands, tools, finished hair from behind/side. No logos or shop signs; identifiable faces flagged "demo image".
